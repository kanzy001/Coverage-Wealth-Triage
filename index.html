Part 1 — The Benchmark

What financially-prepared Singaporeans do

Among working Singaporeans aged 25–45 who've completed this diagnostic, those with the most resilient financial pictures consistently follow one allocation framework. This isn't a rule — it's a reference point to calibrate against.

The savings and investment buckets are separate for a reason: savings is liquid cash accessible in 24 hours. Investments are long-horizon vehicles where you accept short-term volatility for long-term growth. Confusing them is the most common gap we see — money sitting in a savings account that should be compounding.

Part 2 — Critical Illness Coverage

Do you know your Early Critical Illness coverage?

Early CI pays out on early-stage diagnosis — when treatment is most intensive but recovery is still the expected outcome. It's distinct from late-stage CI, which only pays after a serious, often irreversible diagnosis.
